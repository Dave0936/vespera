Our philosophy ↓
Philosophy 01 / 04

Proportion

Long of bonnet, short of tail. The classical grand-tourer stance, drawn by hand at full scale before a single panel is pressed.

Material

Forty metres of hand-waxed thread in every cabin. Each hide is chosen, cut and stitched by one craftsperson, start to finish.

Sound

A naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12, tuned by ear on our own dyno. Hushed at a cruise. Operatic at nine thousand.

Light

One blade of light, front and rear. The Nocturne's signature — recognisable at a quarter mile, after dark.

The Atelier

Four hundred hours,
by hand.

From clay to keys
I — FormWeeks 01–06

Sculpted in clay

Every surface is shaped at full scale and judged by eye under raking light.

II — ColourWeeks 07–09

Fourteen coats

Mixed to your sample, sprayed and flatted by hand between every layer.

III — CabinWeeks 10–14

Nine hides, one hand

Walnut, aluminium and leather, finished by a single trimmer who signs the glovebox.

IV — ChassisWeeks 15–18

Forged, not cast

Aluminium spaceframe, carbon-ceramic brakes, and suspension set up for each owner's roads.

V — ShakedownWeek 19

Three hundred kilometres

Driven through the Mendips at night before a single key is handed over.
